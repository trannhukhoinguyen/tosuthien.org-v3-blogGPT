A kōan (/ˈkoʊæn, -ɑːn/) is a story, dialogue, question, or statement from Chinese Chan Buddhist lore, supplemented with commentaries, that is used in Chan, Zen, Seon and Thiền Buddhist practice in different ways.
The main goal of kōan practice in Zen is to achieve kenshō (Chinese: jianxing 見性), to see or recognize one's buddha-nature.
